LOCAL MARKETS SEE RECORD WEEKEND TRAFFIC

By Sarah Mitchell, Staff Reporter

DOWNTOWN - City officials reported unprecedented visitor numbers at the Central Farmers Market this weekend, with an estimated 15,000 shoppers passing through the historic venue between Saturday and Sunday.

"We've never seen anything like it," said Market Director Thomas Chen in a statement released Monday morning. "The community response has been overwhelming."

The surge in attendance is attributed to several factors, including the annual Harvest Festival, favorable weather conditions, and a new partnership with local food trucks that expanded the market's offerings.

Vendors reported brisk sales across all categories. Fresh produce stands sold out of seasonal items by early afternoon on both days, while artisan bakers saw lines stretching around the block.

"I had to call in extra help," said Maria Gonzalez, owner of Sweet Sunrise Bakery, who has operated a stall at the market for twelve years. "We sold three times our normal inventory."

City Council Member James Wright praised the event's success, noting its positive economic impact on small businesses. "This demonstrates the vital role local markets play in our community's economic ecosystem," Wright said during Tuesday's council meeting.

However, some residents expressed concerns about parking availability and pedestrian congestion. The Department of Transportation has announced it will review traffic management protocols before next month's Winter Market opening.

The Central Farmers Market operates year-round, Saturdays 8 AM to 2 PM and Sundays 9 AM to 1 PM.
